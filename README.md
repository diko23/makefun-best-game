Clean the maze before the heroes arrive.

You are the dungeon office boy and head janitor working the graveyard shift in the underworld. Adventuring parties leave every dungeon wing in absolute shambles. Before the next elite party arrives, you must complete timed cleanup rounds across 5 dungeon wings: mop up toxic slime pools, reset pressure plate traps, restock chest gold, soothe rowdy creatures, and complete wing cleanup missions!

## How to Play
- **Clean the Dungeon**: Walk across the dungeon floor to automatically mop up slime pools, reset traps, restock chests, and soothe creatures with your mop. Reach each wing's quota before the timer runs out!
- **Fast Clean Time Bonus**: Meeting your wing quota immediately concludes the round and awards a generous time bonus of +10 gold and +25 score per second remaining on the clock.
- **Wings & Upgrade Shop**: Clearing a wing's quota opens the Upgrade Shop between wings. Spend earned gold on Broom Speed, Mop Reach Radius, Extra Time (+5s per wing), and Water Bucket douse range, or continue to the next wing.
- **Surprise Events**: Watch out for dynamic hazards from wing 2 onward:
  - **Dragon Sneeze**: Flaming patches ignite the floor. Stepping into fire costs 3 seconds of precious time; douse flames using your water bucket!
  - **Goblin Party**: Rowdy goblins scatter extra slime and trash across the chamber.
  - **Living Slime**: A persistent roaming slime creature that crawls along the floor and re-stains cleaned spots upon contact.
- **Missions**: Each wing assigns an optional cleanup objective (e.g., Clean 3 Slime Pools or Reset 2 Traps) for a +75 gold bonus.

## Controls
- **Move**: Arrow keys or WASD (or click on the field to walk toward a target)
- **Start / Confirm / Continue**: Space, Enter, or Click
- **Water Bucket (Douse Fires)**: B or Shift
- **Mute Audio**: M
- **Pause Game**: P or Escape
