# THE MAZEJANITOR

Clean the maze before the heroes arrive

You are the dungeon office boy and head janitor working the graveyard shift in the underworld. Hero parties have left Wing A in absolute shambles. Before the next elite party arrives, you must complete timed cleanup rounds across 5 dungeon wings: mop up toxic slime pools, reset pressure plate traps, restock chest gold, soothe rowdy creatures, and complete wing missions!

## How to Play
- **Clean the Dungeon**: Move across the dungeon floor and clean up slime pools, traps, chests, and creatures before the timer runs out to meet your quota.
- **Wings & Upgrade Shop**: Clear each wing's quota to enter the Upgrade Shop between wings. Spend earned gold on Broom Speed, Mop Radius, Extra Time (+5s), and Water Bucket upgrades, or continue to the next wing.
- **Surprise Events**: Watch out for random events from wing 2 onward (Dragon Sneeze fire patches doused by bucket, Goblin Party trash, Living Slime).

## Controls
- **Move**: Arrow keys or WASD
- **Clean / Select / Confirm**: Space or Enter / Click
- **Water Bucket / Douse Fire**: B or Shift
- **Mute Audio**: M
- **Pause**: P or Escape
